Grainy film looks (assets/film)

CIE data
assets/film/colorimetry.json holds, resampled to 380-780 nm in 5 nm steps:
- CIE 1931 colour-matching functions, 2 degree observer. CIE (2019). DOI 10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b
- Components of the relative spectral distribution of daylight. CIE (2022). DOI 10.25039/CIE.DS.w7zunnny
Both are published by the International Commission on Illumination (CIE) under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The file also holds three reflectance spectra computed from them (tools/film/colorimetry.py); the file as a whole
is offered under the same licence.

Film and paper data
assets/film/stocks and assets/film/papers hold numbers read from the makers' published technical data sheets:
spectral sensitivities, characteristic curves and dye spectral densities. Each file names its source document,
its address and the checksum of the copy that was read. The looks computed from them are simulations: real film
also depends on its age, development, printing and scanning.
